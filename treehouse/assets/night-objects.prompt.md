## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production pixel art sprite sheet for a dark whimsical treehouse platform game. Wide landscape canvas, THREE separate evenly spaced columns, same ground baseline: left an original creepy mechanical woodland raccoon mascot standing facing right, center SAME raccoon automaton walking facing right, right a small antique brass handheld oil lantern with warm amber flame. Raccoon has jointed wooden and rusty metal limbs, patchwork faded teal costume, striped tail, round ears, pale glass eyes, eerie toy grin. Family-friendly spooky, no blood. Inspired by haunted animatronic atmosphere, ORIGINAL design, no existing franchise character. Crisp 16-bit pixel art, strong outlines and readable silhouettes at 26x38 pixels, lantern at 10x15. Each subject entirely inside its column with generous padding. No floor, text, scene, labels or shadows. True transparent RGBA alpha=0 background, absolutely NO painted checkerboard. Lantern glow only inside glass, no glow outside silhouette.
